How to download the CSV
  1. Log in to the IBKR portal.
  2. Open Performance & Reports, then Statements.
  3. Choose Activity, period Year to date, format CSV.
  4. Download, then upload it here.

Or ask Claude to pull IBKR for you and import the file it returns in Feedback, Claude tab; that file also carries time-weighted performance.

The statement replaces the stored portfolio: sales disappear and buys appear on their own. ETFs are recognised from the statement itself.

Year by year
How the target is set

Target = yearly income × a safe multiple. The multiple grows with the length of retirement (plan-until age minus the age you stop):

30 years or less25×, 4.00% a year
40 years28×, 3.57% a year
50 years or more31×, 3.23% a year

Why stopping younger needs more: stop at 45 and plan to 90, and the money has to last 45 years of withdrawals and market falls. A lower withdrawal rate protects against a crash early in retirement.

How it runs: month by month, your invested capital grows at the expected return (after inflation when the switch is on) and your plan's monthly amount is added. The date is the first month the capital reaches the target for that age.
